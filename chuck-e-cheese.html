Chuck E. Cheese.

A collection of work from my time at Chuck E. Cheese, where I contributed across web, brand systems, and in-store technology.

Role

UI/UX Designer + Marketing Operations

Company

CEC Entertainment (Chuck E. Cheese)

Scope

Web, Kiosk & Campaign Design

Overview

This spans several projects and roles, so I've broken it out by area below — from consumer-facing campaign pages to the self-serve kiosks deployed across 500+ locations.

Campaign Web Designs

Translating creative direction into functional, on-brand pages.

Landing pages and web assets built to support seasonal and promotional campaigns, translating creative direction into functional, on-brand web experiences — designed responsively in collaboration with the web development team.

Full-Service Kiosk Designs

Ordering UX for 500+ locations.

End-to-end ordering and product selection UX for self-serve kiosks deployed across 500+ locations — owning the full experience from browsing to checkout, designed for speed and clarity in a high-traffic, family-facing environment.

Kid Check Landing Page

Communicating trust and clarity to parents.

The landing page for Kid Check, Chuck E. Cheese's child safety and check-in system, designed to communicate trust and clarity to parents at a glance.

Chuck E. Cheese Design System

Keeping a high-volume, multi-team brand consistent.

The design system underpinning Chuck E. Cheese's digital presence, built to keep a high-volume, multi-team brand consistent across web and campaign work. (Website redesign in progress.)
